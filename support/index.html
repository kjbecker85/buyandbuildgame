Help

Buy and Build is a strategy game about private equity. It plays in a browser at buyandbuildgame.com and as an app on a phone or a tablet.

Write to us

Send an email to hello@engagequalia.com. Tell us what you were doing and what happened, and which phone or browser you use. A person reads every email, and we answer within a few days.

Where is my saved game?

The game saves as you play. As a guest, your career and your game are kept on the device you play on, so they are not on your other devices. If you sign in, your career and your saved full fund are kept with your account, and you can pick them up on another device by signing in there.

In a browser, clearing the site's data or using a private window removes a guest's saved game. In the app, deleting the app removes it.

How do I delete my account?

Open the first screen of the game. Under Your account, open Account and choose Delete my account. That deletes your account, your career, your saved game and every score, and it cannot be undone. You can also ask us by email, and we delete it for you.

I hear no sound

The Sound button is at the top of the map. If it says Sound off, tap it. On an iPhone, also check the switch on the side of the phone and the volume.

The map is slow or does not show

The map is drawn in 3D. On an old phone or computer it can be slow. The game still works without it: every town is also on the grid on the turn page.

For a class or a team

A class or a team can play the same market and compare results with a group code. See the page for teams and classes.
