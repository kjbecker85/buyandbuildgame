Privacy

Private Equity Game. Last changed on November 22, 2026.

You can play the whole game as a guest. A guest's progress stays in the browser on your device. A guest sends us only the anonymous counts described below.

What we keep when you sign in

  • Your email address. We use it only to sign you in.
  • Your password, if you made one. It is stored scrambled (hashed), so nobody can read it, us included.
  • If you sign in with Google: your email address and your Google account id. We ask Google for nothing else.
  • The name, fund name and game piece you choose.
  • Your career, your saved full fund, and the result of each game you finish.

Game news by email

On the front page, and at the end of a game played as a guest, you can give your email to get game news. We store that email, where you gave it, and the date. We use it only to send game news, a few emails a year. We do not use it to sign you in.

It can be removed on request. Reply to any email from us and ask, and we delete it from the list.

Who can see it

The leaderboard shows your name, your fund name, your game piece and your best scores to everyone. Nobody else can see your email or your saved games.

What we count

We use PostHog, an analytics service, to count how the game is played. It counts these moments and nothing else:

  • A page of the site or the game opened.
  • On the front page: a tap on Enter Game or Watch Video.
  • In the game: a game started, a company bought, an add-on bought, a company sold, a game finished, and an account created.
  • On the front page and in the game: an email given for game news. The count carries where it was given, never the email.

Each count carries a few game facts, such as the mode, the industry, the place among the three funds and whether the fund beat the investors' line. It never carries your email, your password or a name you type.

The counts are anonymous until you sign in. A random id kept in your browser groups them. Once you sign in, they are tied to your account id, which is a random number, not your email. Signing out goes back to a new random id.

We do not record your screen, and we do not count every tap. If your browser asks sites not to track you (Do Not Track), nothing is counted.

What we do not do

We do not use advertising, sell data, or share it.

Where it is stored

Accounts and the list for game news are stored with Supabase, a database service, on servers in the United States. The counts are stored with PostHog, also in the United States.

Deleting your account

Sign in, open Account on the title page, and choose Delete my account. This deletes your email, your progress and every score at once.
