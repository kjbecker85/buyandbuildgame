A private equity strategy game

Buy and Build

Buy it. Build it. Sell it.

You run a $50 million fund. Buy a company in Dallas/Fort Worth, buy the towns around it before two rival funds do, and choose the moment to sell.

Enter Game

Free. Plays in your browser on a computer or a phone. About 25 minutes.

  1. Buy a company

    Pick one of three companies for sale, check the books, and decide how much the bank should lend.

  2. Build a territory

    Every town on the map holds one company. Buy it first and the town is yours. Two rival funds want the same towns.

  3. Sell, and beat the line

    Read real financial statements every quarter. When buyers make offers, choose your moment. Finish above the investors' line and ahead of the rivals.

Your mentor in the game is Adam Coffey, author of The Private Equity Playbook.
