Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The whole game is free to play and has no ads.

How long does a game take?

A quick deal is one company and takes about 25 minutes. The full fund is ten years of deals and takes 60 to 90 minutes. It saves as you go.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play the whole game as a guest. An account keeps your career on every device and puts your best score on the leaderboard.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It plays in the browser on a phone, a tablet or a computer. There is nothing to install.

Do I need to know finance?

No. Each idea is explained in plain words at the moment you need it.

What will I learn?

How a private equity deal works from the purchase to the sale: what a loan does to the return, why add-ons raise the price a buyer pays, how to read the three financial statements, and when to sell. The game teaches how deals work. It does not give investment advice.

Can I use it with a team or a class?

Yes. Make a group in the game, share its link, and a private leaderboard ranks your players. See the page for teams and classes.
